SunlightOS converts the official monochrome TrueType outlines at build time to
grayscale alpha masks in its MiniType MTF2 format. The generated derivative
font is exposed as “Sun Emoji”, remains monochrome, and is tinted at runtime by
the current text color. No OpenMoji color artwork is included.

The OpenMoji artwork and converted Sun Emoji asset are licensed separately
from the SunlightOS kernel and application source code. See LICENSE.txt in this
directory for the complete upstream license and attribution information.
