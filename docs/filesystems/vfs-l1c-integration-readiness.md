## How to read the evidence

The debug adapter can hold and release replies, inject preparation failure and arrange exact service refusal. Its normal-exit hook takes the fixture adapter after exit commits, drops the local child owner outside scheduler and adapter guards, and leaves registration settlement to polling and the authenticated protocol. It proves autonomous disposal for two checkpoints. It does not define what the ordinary adapters must do after a captured parent exits or replaces its image. Publication fencing alone neither disposes an abandoned handle nor proves remote cleanup. The two owners and their lifetimes need explicit production handling.

The original primary 4-vCPU heap samples rose from **280,808 B** to **2,607,752 B**. Subtracting **2,326,784 B** of measured process-vector, run-queue and empty descriptor-table capacity from the **2,326,944 B** increase leaves **160 B unattributed**. The warmed total remained stable at cycles 1, 10, 100 and 1,000. A separate traced boot started 64 B higher and ended 96 B lower, leaving no residual in that run. Neither its variable 96 B IPC buffer nor its telemetry and rounding observations identify the original allocations. This is an open attribution item, with no evidence of cumulative growth or a transaction leak in the measured serial workload. It blocks declaring full heap accounting complete; it does not independently demonstrate a reason to change scheduler allocation, swap, or publication behavior.

Primary accounting images, candidate images and production release builds are separate witnesses. The isolated candidate uses documented source reductions; a comparison of covered files does not make the boot images equivalent. The candidate guard and Phase 3.0 passes cannot clear the four primary findings. Likewise, the standard SWAP-1 pass verifies its independent pressure workload, not fault-driven swap-in in a creation transaction. Keep the standard four-vCPU, two-pool configuration and the existing lower-CPU pool policy.

## Minimum remaining work

1. Close the parent lifecycle matrix with exact identity and autonomous progress, and reconcile the Stage 1 reply-ticket/source-pin contract with production adapter disposal. Keep the two qualified debug exit cases as controls.
2. Obtain a trusted successor witness, reuse the retired row across service lifetimes, and reject stale replies without weakening provenance or recycling unresolved ownership.
3. Close the outstanding resource classes and heap census with exact owners. Preserve the historical 160 B as unattributed unless evidence can identify it; report any new matched measurements separately.
4. Resolve production admission, deadline/drain, ABI and bootstrap contracts before activation. When separately authorized, wire both ordinary adapters and qualify the actual success and failure paths, including integrated mixed and race coverage.
5. Clear the primary ownership gate and qualify ordinary boot and both interactive applications on the primary platform. Retain the original regression gates, including standard 4-vCPU SWAP-1, for the integrated source.

The current review does not authorize implementation or change any acceptance threshold. Reuse the recorded scoped passes when their source and scope still apply.
