The Public Suffix List is from https://publicsuffix.org/list/public_suffix_list.dat,
licensed under MPL-2.0 (https://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/). Its original header carries
its upstream version. public_suffix_ascii.txt derives from that pinned snapshot
by removing comments and converting labels with Python's IDNA codec. Both ICANN
and private suffixes are retained. The runtime does no network lookup.
