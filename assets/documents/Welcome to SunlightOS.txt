Hello, and welcome.

You are now inside SunlightOS, a small operating system with a bright
desktop, a growing set of native apps, and a core named SunlightX.

This Documents folder belongs to you. Keep your notes, drafts, personal
files, and project documents here.

SunlightOS is built with a simple goal:
to be understandable, useful, fast, and friendly.

Places to explore:

- Pictures
  Contains sample pictures and wallpapers.

- Documents
  A place for notes, text files, and personal work.

- Files
  Browse your folders and the system.

- Control Panel
  Change wallpaper and other system settings.

- Terminal
  Explore SunlightOS from the inside.

Thank you for trying SunlightOS.

Enjoy your new little sun-powered machine.
