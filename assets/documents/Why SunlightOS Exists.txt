+----------------------------------+
|       Why SunlightOS Exists      |
+----------------------------------+

SunlightOS exists to explore a simple idea:

A modern operating system can be small, understandable, fast, open, and
intelligent without becoming heavy or chaotic.

SunlightOS is built around a microkernel-oriented design and small UNIX-like
components. Each part should do one job well. The shell should be a shell. A
service manager should manage services. Calendar should be a calendar. Files
should manage files. The system should be composed from clear pieces instead
of one giant component quietly doing everything.

But SunlightOS is not only about architecture.

Good concepts matter, but an operating system also needs a visible daily
experience. Users should be able to open files, write notes, manage events,
change settings, receive notifications, and understand what the system is
doing. Strong ideas should become real workflows.

SunlightOS is future-first. It is not trying to recreate the past. It is
trying to start the future early: a compact, modern, local-first desktop
where apps and services can cooperate through clean system layers.

sunlight-kv is one example of this direction. Small persistent state, such as
calendar events, settings, notifications, tasks, and recent items, can live
in a shared local store instead of every app inventing its own fragile file
format and polling loop. When Calendar saves an event, the desktop can see
it. Later, Tasks, Notifications, and WiseOwl can understand it too.

WiseOwl is the long-term assistant layer for SunlightOS. The goal is not a
chatbot glued on top of the desktop. The goal is local intelligence woven
into the workflow: reminders, task help, event awareness, careful automation,
and useful actions that respect the user and the system.

SunlightOS is for people who like minimalism, performance, open source
software, clean boundaries, and modern user experience. It is for people who
believe a system can be understandable without being primitive, and
intelligent without being bloated.

SunlightOS is experimental. It is growing one working piece at a time.

Small tools.
Clear services.
Modern desktop.
Local intelligence.
A future that starts early.
